How Digital Electronic Voting System Works

Four simple stages guaranteeing verified eligibility, anonymous ballot submission, and immutable ledger recording.

1

Voter Identity Verification

The elector verifies their National EPIC ID and authenticates with an Aadhaar-linked OTP.

2

Blockchain Whitelisting

The smart contract issues eligibility to the voter's public address without storing personal credentials.

3

EVM Digital Ballot & Confirmation

Voter presses the blue button; the digital confirmation window presents a 7-second audit slip with candidate and transaction hash.

4

Block Consensus

The smart contract increments candidate vote count and permanently marks the voter address as completed.
